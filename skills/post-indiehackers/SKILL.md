# IndieHackers Adapter

Facts live in `platforms/indiehackers.json` — status is `degraded`: the
community has cooled since acquisition and the product changes without
notice. Draft export is the reliable path.

## What IH rewards (culture)

- Transparency with real numbers: revenue, users, timelines, churn.
- "What worked / what didn't" breakdowns — the community trades tactics.
- Maker-voiced honesty over startup-investor polish.

## Drafting from the content graph

- `core-claim` → milestone title with the real number in it.
- `proof` → the metrics and details — this is where IH lives.
- `technical`/`context` → how you got there; specifics beat story arcs.
- `cta` → "ask me anything" — the community answers questions with care.
- Posts are filed under topic groups: the draft names the target group
  and the user confirms fit.

## Publishing

- `manual` only — posting flow and bot detection shift without notice.
- No public API.

## Genuine engagement

- Answer every question on your post; IH rewards presence.
- Share numbers because they help others, not because they impress.

## Pitfalls

- Verify the group/post flow still matches expectations before trusting
  any older automation notes — the platform drifts.
